About SP Engineering & Consultancy

SP Engineering Consultancy is a professionally managed Design & Build MEP contracting and liaisoning firm, established in 2019 and based in Chennai, India.

Formed by a team of experienced professionals with more than two decades of combined industry expertise, we specialize in delivering reliable, compliant, and cost-effective Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, and Fire Fighting solutions across residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

  • ✔ Design & Build MEP Contracting
  • ✔ Electrical, Mechanical & PHE Systems
  • ✔ TNEB & Statutory Liaison Services
  • ✔ Safety, Compliance & Quality Focus

Our Vision

To become a trusted market leader in MEP contracting and related services by consistently delivering engineering excellence, integrity, and innovation through committed leadership and teamwork.

Our Mission

To establish SP Engineering Consultancy as a dependable MEP service partner by delivering technically sound designs, zero-defect execution, safe installations, and complete client satisfaction.

Our Expertise

Our multidisciplinary expertise allows us to deliver integrated MEP solutions tailored to project requirements, industry standards, and statutory compliance.

  • Electrical Systems: HT & LT electrical works, internal and external installations, TNEB liaisoning, approvals, switchgear installation, testing, commissioning, solar systems, CCTV, access control, and structured cabling.
  • Mechanical Systems: Fire Fighting systems including hydrant, sprinkler, FM200, fire alarm systems, HVAC and VRF systems, basement ventilation, and ducting works.
  • Plumbing & PHE Systems: Water supply systems, drainage and storm water systems, sub-soil drainage, and hydropneumatic solutions.

Leadership & Team

SP Engineering Consultancy is led by qualified and experienced professionals with strong technical and managerial backgrounds.

Our leadership team is supported by skilled design engineers, execution heads, site engineers, supervisors, and field staff, ensuring structured coordination, quality control, and successful project execution.
