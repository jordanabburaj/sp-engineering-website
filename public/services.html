Our Services

Electrical Engineering

SP Engineering Consultancy delivers comprehensive electrical engineering services. Our scope includes internal and external electrical works, HT & LT systems, HT trenchless cable pulling, and switchgear installation, testing, and commissioning. We also handle electrical design, load calculations, power distribution, and safety compliance for all project types.

We provide complete TNEB liaisoning for HT & LT services, including load sanction approvals, name transfer, and CEIG clearances. Our services extend to CCTV and access control systems, structured network cabling and racking, and solar solutions for domestic and commercial applications.

Plumbing Systems (PHE)

Our plumbing and public health engineering services focus on efficient water management, hygiene, and long-term system reliability. We provide erection and commissioning of water supply systems, hydropneumatic systems, and complete sanitary installations aligned with statutory and project requirements.

Our scope also includes erection and commissioning of drainage systems, storm water systems, and sub-soil drainage systems. We ensure proper system design, coordination, and execution support for efficient flow management, safety, and durability.

Mechanical Works (HVAC & Fire Fighting)

We deliver integrated mechanical engineering solutions covering HVAC, ventilation, and fire-fighting systems aligned with architectural and operational requirements. Our services include erection and commissioning of all types of AC units, including VRF systems, and basement car parking ventilation with complete ducting systems.

Our fire-fighting scope covers erection and commissioning of hydrant systems, sprinkler systems, FM200 systems, and both conventional and addressable fire alarm systems. We provide complete system coordination, statutory approvals, and execution support to ensure safety, compliance, and efficiency.

Solar Power Systems

We deliver comprehensive solar power solutions for on-grid and off-grid systems, supporting residential, commercial, and industrial applications. Our scope includes system assessment, capacity planning, PV module and inverter selection, and overall system coordination to ensure optimal performance and energy efficiency.

We provide complete support for installation and commissioning, grid connectivity and net metering assistance, regulatory and statutory approvals, and performance compliance. Our solutions are designed to deliver long-term reliability, reduced energy costs, and sustainable power generation.

TNEB Liaisoning & Statutory Approvals

We provide comprehensive TNEB liaisoning and statutory approval services for electrical infrastructure projects. Our scope includes site survey reports, load estimation, online application processing, and coordination with authorities for temporary EB services up to 110 kW and permanent HT & LT connections based on self-execution methodology.

We handle sanction approvals for EHT, HT, and LT services, erection coordination for substations and distribution systems, CEIG inspections, CIG approvals, and final statutory clearances. Our services ensure full regulatory compliance and smooth project execution without delays.
